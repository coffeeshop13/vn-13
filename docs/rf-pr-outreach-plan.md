# RF PR and outreach plan for VN13 / VN-13

## Positioning

VN13 should be pitched in Russia as two connected stories:

- VN13 as a distribution partner for European womenswear brands and boutiques.
- VN-13 as an own womenswear brand focused on limited capsule collections, premium materials, and controlled distribution.

## Practical budget tiers

Lean start: 80 000-150 000 RUB per month

- 2 niche/editorial placements.
- 2-3 SEO article placements through marketplaces or direct outreach.
- 1 interview/comment placement attempt.
- Best for testing messaging and indexing.

Working start: 180 000-350 000 RUB per month

- 4-6 relevant placements.
- 1 stronger retail/business/fashion article or interview.
- 2-3 mid-tier SEO placements.
- 1-2 partner/showroom/brand links.
- Best for first 3 months.

Aggressive but still controlled: 400 000-700 000 RUB per month

- 8-12 placements.
- 1 stronger media placement.
- 3-5 niche placements.
- 3-5 SEO/supporting links.
- Best after analytics confirm which pages gain impressions.

## Anchor rules

Monthly anchor mix:

- 40% branded: `VN13`, `VN-13`, `бренд VN-13`.
- 25% naked/natural URLs.
- 20% partial commercial anchors.
- 10% informational anchors.
- 5% exact commercial anchors.

Exact commercial anchors should be rare:

- `женская одежда`
- `эксклюзивная женская одежда`
- `производитель женской одежды`

## Do not buy

- Links from unrelated gambling/crypto/adult/article-farm sites.
- Foreign cheap placements without Russian-language relevance.
- Sitewide footer/sidebar links.
- Exact-match anchors in every placement.
- Pages closed from indexing or marked noindex.
- Duplicate spun articles across many domains.

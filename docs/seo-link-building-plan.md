# VN13 SEO Link Building Plan

## Goal

Build a natural backlink and mention profile for two positioning lines:

- VN13 as a fashion distribution partner for European womenswear brands.
- VN-13 as an own womenswear brand and manufacturer of limited capsule collections.

## Monthly Baseline

- 5-10 quality placements per month for the first 6 months.
- 2-3 brand mentions without exact-match anchors.
- 1-2 expert/interview placements from the founder or buying/distribution perspective.
- 1 partner or supplier page link when possible.

Avoid packages with hundreds of low-quality links, sitewide footer links, hacked pages, casino/adult neighborhoods, generated blogs, and exact-match anchor spam.

## Anchor Mix

Use a conservative anchor distribution:

- 35% branded: `VN13`, `VN-13`, `VN13 fashion distribution`, `бренд VN-13`.
- 25% URL/natural: `https://vn-13.com`, `сайт VN13`, `подробнее на сайте`.
- 20% partial commercial: `эксклюзивная женская одежда VN-13`, `дистрибуция женской одежды`, `женская одежда для бутиков`.
- 10% informational: `как выбрать женскую одежду для бутика`, `предзаказ коллекций одежды`, `производство женской одежды малыми партиями`.
- 10% partner/context anchors: names of brands, collections, lookbook, interviews, distribution notes.

Exact anchors like `женская одежда` or `эксклюзивная женская одежда` should be used rarely and only on relevant, editorial-quality pages.

## Placement Types

Priority 1:

- Fashion media and independent style publications.
- Business media covering fashion retail, boutique buying, import/distribution, or entrepreneurship.
- Interviews with VN13 about distribution, European brands, and VN-13 as a womenswear label.
- Partner pages from boutiques, showrooms, brands, and manufacturers.

Priority 2:

- Telegram channels with indexed web mirrors or article pages.
- Design, slow fashion, sustainability, and boutique retail blogs.
- Local business directories with clean profiles and a real editorial standard.

Priority 3:

- Niche catalog listings when they allow a clean company profile and link.
- Event pages, showroom announcements, lookbook releases, capsule collection announcements.

## Quality Checklist

Before buying or approving a placement, check:

- The page is indexable and not blocked by robots/noindex.
- The article is thematically relevant to fashion, retail, design, business, or manufacturing.
- The link is inside editorial text, not in a footer/sidebar block.
- The donor site has real traffic or visible audience signals.
- The donor does not publish obvious link-farm articles across unrelated niches.
- The article includes brand context, not only a naked keyword link.

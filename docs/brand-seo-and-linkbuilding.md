# Brand SEO and Link Building

## Target positioning

VN13 should present the portfolio as curated premium womenswear with limited availability, not as a generic reseller list.

Primary commercial message:

- premium women’s clothing
- limited series
- selected European and Asian designers
- boutique wholesale and retail distribution
- own VN-13 line alongside partner brands

## Priority brand pages

1. Manuelle Guibal
2. H+ Hannoh Wessel
3. Moyuru
4. Shoto

7. MJ Watson

Each page should answer:

- What is the brand?
- Why is it relevant to VN13?
- Who should buy it?
- What search intent does it satisfy?

## Link building targets

Best backlink sources:

- brand websites
- showroom websites
- boutique stockist pages
- fashion fair / trade show listings
- designer interviews and press mentions
- partner newsletters and lookbooks

## Outreach angles

- stockist announcement
- curated brand feature
- pre-order availability
- boutique assortment partnership
- limited-series designer selection

## Anchor text direction

Use natural anchors, not exact-match spam.

Good examples:

- VN13 brand page
- premium women’s clothing collection
- boutique distribution partner
- limited-series womenswear
- designer portfolio

## Content support

The journal should publish supporting pieces around:

- why limited series sell better in premium retail
- how VN13 curates designer brands
- how boutiques reduce risk with pre-order
- what premium womenswear buyers look for
